# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical choices constructively,
welcome differing experience levels and keep criticism focused on the work.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing private information
are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately through the [maintainer](https://github.com/likehopper).
Do not publish personal details in an issue. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate
content or restrict participation, considering the circumstances and privacy of those involved.
